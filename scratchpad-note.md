# A Small Note

Disk space is a quiet resource. You rarely think about it until it runs
out, and then it becomes the only thing you can think about. A tool that
maps where the bytes went turns that anxious moment into a solvable one:
you see the shape of the problem, point at the largest block, and reclaim
it.

That is the whole appeal of a good visualization. It does not add
information so much as arrange it, so the answer you needed was there all
along, just waiting for the right frame.
