# A Short Note

Every directory tells a story about how space gets used. Some folders grow
quietly over months, a few kilobytes at a time, until one day they hold more
than the person who created them ever intended. Others balloon overnight when a
single build spills its cache across the disk. The interesting part is never the
total number at the bottom of the report. It is the shape of the tree that leads
there, the branches that carry the weight, and the small leaves that add up when
nobody is counting.

Tools that measure disk usage are, in a sense, storytellers. They walk the tree,
gather the sizes, and then decide how to present what they found so that a person
can understand it at a glance. A good measurement is honest about scale. A good
visualization makes the honest measurement easy to read. When both hold true,
the answer to "where did all my space go" stops being a mystery and becomes a
simple picture.

This file exists for no reason other than to be written. It runs nothing, tests
nothing, and changes no behavior. It is just a few sentences set down on a quiet
branch, a small mark left behind to say that something was here.
